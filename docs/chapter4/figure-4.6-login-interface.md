## Desktop Login Interface

Figure 4.6 shows the desktop login interface of the Inventory Plus web application. The left panel presents the system identity and purpose, while the right panel contains the controls for account access.

1. **Inventory Plus branding** identifies the application and introduces its purpose.
2. **System introduction** explains that the application tracks inventory, sales, orders, and payments for a hardware store.
3. **Username field** accepts the user's account name.
4. **Forgot password link** opens the account recovery process.
5. **Password field** accepts the account password. The eye icon shows or hides the entered password.
6. **Sign in button** submits the entered credentials for authentication.
7. **Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links** provide access to the application's policy information.
