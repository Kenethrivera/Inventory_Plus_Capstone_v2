Figure 4.11 shows the Inventory Plus admin Inventory interface in the product list and product details views. (1) The selected navigation item opens Inventory. (2) The summary shows the product count and total retail value on hand. (3) The stock filters show all, low, critical, and dead stock products. (4) The search field and category filter help locate products. (5) The product table displays each item's category, location, stock coverage, price, and status. (6) New product opens the form for adding an inventory item. (7) The product details panel shows the selected item's name, SKU, availability, and price. (8) The stock summary shows quantity on hand, units sold in seven days, and estimated stock coverage. (9) The tabs open the item's details, location, and stock history. (10) The identity fields show the product's name, SKU, category, manufacturer, model, size, and price. (11) The bottom controls provide a QR label option and a Save changes button.
