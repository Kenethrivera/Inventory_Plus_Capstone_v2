Figure 4.8 shows the Inventory Plus admin Orders & activity interface. (1) The selected navigation item opens the Orders & activity page. (2) The tabs switch between orders, payments, and stock movements. (3) The search field helps locate records by order number, product, staff member, or GCash reference. (4) The filters narrow the list by order status, assigned staff, and time period. (5) The orders table displays each order’s reference, creation time, item count, payment method, cashier, and assigned staff. (6) The status column shows the current stage of each order. (7) The total column displays the amount of each order.
