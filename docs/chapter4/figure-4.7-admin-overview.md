Figure 4.7 shows the Inventory Plus admin Overview interface in its upper and lower sections. (1) The navigation menu provides access to the system’s main pages. (2) The store status shows whether the store is open and when it closes. (3) The sales summary displays today’s sales, order count, payment breakdown, and sales chart. (4) The order queue groups orders by their current stage. (5) The notification bell displays the current notification count. (6) The approvals panel shows requests awaiting review. (7) The stock alerts panel identifies items with low or zero stock. (8) “Waiting for staff” shows orders that have not yet been taken by staff. (9) “Being prepared” shows orders currently in preparation. (10) “Ready for release” shows orders prepared for handover. (11) Recent POS activity lists recent sales, including their time, order reference, payment method, and amount.
